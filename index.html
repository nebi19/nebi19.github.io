Featured · Personal project · Applied AI

Offline AI Study Assistant

An AI tutor for Ethiopian Grade 7 and 8 mathematics that runs entirely on one school-lab computer, with no internet. Students on lab PCs or tablets connect over the local network.

  • Answers come only from the textbook (retrieval-augmented generation) and cite the unit, section and page. If the answer isn't in the book, it says so.
  • Visual unit summaries (concept map, key formulas, worked example) and 5-question quizzes, each answer checked by a calculator and a second blind answer before it's shown.
  • English and Amharic (NLLB-200 translation), OCR for scanned textbook pages, and a teacher view of common questions and quiz scores.
  • No logins and no student data leaves the machine. Covered by 95 automated tests.
